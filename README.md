## Research & experience

### 🔬 KAUST: Stochastic Numerics Group (Prof. Raúl Tempone)
*Research intern · Feb–Aug 2026 · Thuwal, Saudi Arabia*

- Implemented and evaluated, in **PyTorch**, a mesh-free framework for **high-dimensional stochastic optimal control** based on regularized Hamiltonians and problem-specific approximation functions.
- Applied it to **LQG control**, **Merton portfolio optimization** and **Bermudan max-call option pricing**.
- Pushed Bermudan max-call pricing to dimensions beyond the reach of grid-based PDE methods, with results consistent with benchmarks from the literature.
